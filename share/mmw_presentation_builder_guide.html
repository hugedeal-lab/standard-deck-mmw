Getting started

Paste your content and say what the deck is for. The more it knows about the audience and length, the less you'll adjust later.

Give it:

  • Your content: pasted copy, a rough outline, notes, or existing slides to convert.
  • The purpose and audience: for example "client pitch", "internal production review" or "Q3 results for leadership".
  • A rough length if you have one. Otherwise it sizes the deck from your content.
  • Any photos you want used. Without them it suggests shots for you to add later.
  • Real numbers only. It never invents statistics, quotes or sources, so give it the data you want charted.

Example requests:

  • "Build a 10-slide pitch deck for the CX-90 fall campaign from the brief below. Audience is the Mazda brand team."
  • "Turn this shot list into storyboard slides, then add casting and location pages."
  • "Make Meta 4:5 carousel and TikTok video spec sheets for these three assets."
  • "Chart this spend data by channel and add a US map of the launch markets."

Not sure where to start? Type help in the chat. It explains what it can do, what to give it and how to edit, and can list its slide layouts.

How a build works

Decks of 8 slides or fewer build straight away. For 9 or more, you approve an outline first.

  1. Outline. It shows a table with each slide's layout and content, plus notes on long copy and any photo wells to fill. Reply "proceed" or ask for changes, such as "merge slides 4 and 5" or "use a photo divider for section 2".
  2. Build. It creates the deck as a preview in chat.
  3. Tour. Right after the preview, it summarises the deck (slide by slide for short decks, section by section for long ones). It then lists anything to check: changes from the outline, copy it trimmed or moved, and photos to add. It ends with next steps.
  4. Preview. Use the arrows or your keyboard's arrow keys to page through. Notes shows the speaker notes it wrote for each slide.
  5. Download. The Download button saves an editable PowerPoint file named after the deck.

Photos

Some slides fill themselves with MMW template photography; the rest show grey photo boxes until you add pictures.

KindSlidesWhat you see
Fill themselvesPhoto covers, photo dividers, photo headline statements, platform dividersFinished slides with template photography
Photo boxesEditorial photo + copy, storyboards, casting, locations, moodboards, social spec sheetsGrey boxes reading "Right-click → change picture"

When you haven't supplied photos, it keeps photo boxes to slides whose copy describes something visual, such as a vehicle, place, person, event or piece of creative. It suggests a shot for each box in the outline and the tour.

To add your own photos:

  • Before the build: attach them in chat and say which slide each belongs on.
  • After download: in PowerPoint, right-click a photo box and choose Change Picture. It crops to fit the box like the template's own photos.

Template photography on the self-filling slides can be replaced the same way.

Copy length

Every text box has a character limit measured from the template, so long copy gets a roomier layout, a split or a trim, and it tells you which.

  • Layout choice: long copy goes to a layout with more body room. The outline marks these, for example "long copy, ~450 chars".
  • Copy notes: after the build, it adds one line for each slide where it changed the layout for length, trimmed or paraphrased your copy, split it across slides, or let it render slightly smaller.
  • Want your exact words? Say so. It will split copy across slides rather than trim it.
  • Big statements and headlines are short by design: a few words at display size.

Social spec sheets take full 500-character post copy and 100-character headlines; the type shrinks to fit.

Editing and finishing

Ask for changes in chat by slide number, then download again; finish small touches in PowerPoint.

  • In chat: "Shorten slide 3's headline", "Swap slide 6 to a dark background", "Add a slide after 8 comparing the two scripts", "Skip speaker notes". It rebuilds the deck each time, so download the latest version.
  • In PowerPoint: every text box, chart and table is editable, and photo boxes take your own pictures.
  • The PowerPoint file is the final version. The chat preview is a close approximation; line breaks and spacing can differ slightly.
  • Fonts: install Mazda Type and Mazda Type Bold before opening the file, or PowerPoint substitutes another font and spacing will look off.
  • Footers: there is no "Company Confidential" footer or page-number option; slides carry only the template's own marks.
Need source assets? Icons, diagram pieces, and the rest of the raw MMW template live in the MMW Deck Templates folder in Box. Use it if you want to work directly from the source template rather than through the builder.

Troubleshooting

Most problems are fixed by asking for a rebuild in a fresh chat.

ProblemFix
The preview is blankAsk it to rebuild the deck. A small error in a build can stop every slide drawing.
One slide is empty or looks sparseTell it which slide; it may be missing content for that layout.
A message says some images were left outThey failed to download. Download again, or add them in PowerPoint.
Text looks wrong in PowerPointInstall Mazda Type and Mazda Type Bold, then reopen the file.
Copy was shortenedCheck the copy notes in the tour, or ask for your exact words split across slides.
A fix you were promised isn't showingStart a new chat; older chats can keep using an earlier version of the builder.

Tips

  • Make one number stand out: ask for the chart with a highlight, e.g. "column chart of CX-90 vs four competitors, highlight CX-90". The highlighted bar is Spark tan and the rest are grey.
  • Brand colors in charts: ask for Canopy (green), Tide (blue) or Aurora (purple) to match a section's mood. Otherwise charts use the template palette.
  • Contacts on the closing slide: include names and emails in your request and they go on the thank-you slide.
  • Exact copy: say "keep my exact words" and the builder splits copy across slides instead of trimming.

Support & feedback

Questions, feature requests, or something that didn't come out right — send it to Zack Jemas at zack.jemas@vml.com.

Feedback helps most when it includes four things:

  1. The deck file the builder made — the HTML from the chat, not just the PowerPoint. It shows which version of the builder made it.
  2. The PowerPoint, if the problem shows up there.
  3. A screenshot of the slide, with its slide number.
  4. What you asked for, copied from the chat, and what you expected instead.

Also useful: slides that work well, layouts you expected but didn't get, and anything you had to fix by hand in PowerPoint.
